## What are some of the drawbacks to not doing enough requirements gathering? What could go wrong?
>If you don't do enough requirements gathering, you may not be giving the client what they actually want. Clients may or may not have the technical expertise needed to fully elaborate on everything that is required for a project (and if the project is of a large scope, technical expertise may not mean they know everything anyways). Not gathering enough requirements may backfire in many ways. The client may assume that certain features or limitations were to be included, whereas the developer didn't do so. It may also lead to the buildup of technical debt which can be extraordinarily costly to reconstruct.
