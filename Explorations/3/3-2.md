# CS 361 - Exploration 3.2: Wireframes

## Why aren't we generally concerned with making the wireframes look aesthetically-pleasing?

Wireframes are purely for functionality, not aesthetics. It's more concerned with whether a button needs to be somewhere, or a dialog box needs to be presented somewhere else than how that element actually looks.

## Under what circumstances would you want mockups that show the screens in pixel-perfect detail?

If you wanted something to present to a client (or other non-developer stakeholder), you might be more inclined to go into more detail.
