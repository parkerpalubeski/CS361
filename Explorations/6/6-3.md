# Imagine you're about to start a fixed-deadline project. How might you use WBS in concert with CPM in scheduling?
A WBS actually seems like it would make a great blueprint for CPM, by allowing you to schedule pieces of the WBS.
# Does the measure of the team's velocity become more or less accurate over time?
It definitely becomes more accurate closer to the end, but in the early phases I imagine it might actually become even worse than the original estimate.
# Even with velocity measurements, there's always some inaccuracy. If you were building a schedule, how would you deal with this inaccuracy?
You might be better off moving with something like a rolling average than a single-point metric. That way it gives you an idea of trends while giving a bit less credence to major outliers.
