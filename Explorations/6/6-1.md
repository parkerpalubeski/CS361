# Come up with a quick and dirty time estimate for the complete bike shop project. Use 2x as your multiplier.
I would estimate for a single developer of my skill level, with some level of automation, this project would likely take somewhere between 4-6 weeks. So the dirty estimate would be ~ 8-12 weeks. This is for a full implementation, not just a prototype.

# Contrast story points and T-shirt sizes as methods of estimating relative task size. Benefits and drawbacks?
T shirt size seems like an excellent way to abstract and simplify the estimation of a task. But it's far too vague for you to calculate precise time estimates from tasks that are simply XS-XL. Story points basically seem more precise and less ambiguous, but it probably takes longer to reach a concensus on what score to give a task with story points.
