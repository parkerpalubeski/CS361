# Suppose you ask an AI assistant to implement a function that transfers money between two accounts. Explain how stating invariants in your prompt changes what you can expect from the output.
* Invariants are essential to include in the prompts. If you don't state them, the AI might assume what the invariants are based on what is common in implementations of similar code, but invariants are likely often specific to the business itself and thus difficult, if not impossible, to guess like an AI would abe forced to without including them in the prompt. If the invariants are included in the prompt, it won't have to guess on what it needs to account for.

# Does stating invariants in the design doc obviate the need for testing? Why or why not?
* No, because the AI can occasionally get things wrong, or the way the invariant was prompted might still introduce some ambiguity that could lead to some edge cases. Some invariants are harmless, but some are related to compliance laws or core business practices. Testing is the only way to be 100% sure that the output is as intended.
