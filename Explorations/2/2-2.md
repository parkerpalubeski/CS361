# CS 361 - Exploration 2.2: User Scenarios

## Imagine a system that allows airlines and customers to manage reservations and tickets. Come up with a user scenario for that system.

Caitlin, 21, is a college student at a University in another city from her family home. She is seeking to fly back home for thanksgiving, but was so caught up with school-work that she put it off until the last minute. She needs to quickly find an available flight home. However, as a chemistry student, her skills with technology are fairly limited. Her scenario highlights the importance of responsiveness and ease of use. If she encounters too much friction from the app in being matched with a flight home, she might become one *very* unhappy customer.
