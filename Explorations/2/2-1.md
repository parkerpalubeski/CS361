# CS 361 - Exploration 2.1: Functional Specs

## Speculate on why the functional spec doesn't describe how the system is to be coded up, but only describes what the system is supposed to do. What's the benefit for splitting it apart that way?

The main purpose of this document seems to lean more towards what the front-end user experience will be like than the technologies for using it in the back-end. I suppose it's best to be split like that since the software can often be viewed as having "two experiences", that of the developers, maintainers and business personnel, who would have access to the back-end, and that of the clients/customers, who have access to the front-end. Essentially, you are splitting it between the two stacks that most software projects have.
